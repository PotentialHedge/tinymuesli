tinyMuesli

Takes notes in your meetings so you remember them. It records and transcribes everything on your Mac, and nothing leaves it.

Download for macOS Source on GitHub 
curl -fsSL https://github.com/PotentialHedge/tinymuesli/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sh

Or brew install --cask potentialhedge/tap/tinymuesli. Free and open source. Needs macOS 26 on an Apple Silicon Mac. Early version, so expect rough edges.

Notices your meetings

When Zoom, Teams, Slack, FaceTime or a call in your browser uses the mic, one click starts recording. No calendar access needed.

Knows who said what

You on the mic and everyone else on system audio, with on-device speaker identification at the end.

Fully local

Apple's on-device speech recognition. Audio is deleted after transcribing unless you keep it.

Plain files

Each meeting is a folder with transcript.md and transcript.json. Your files, your tools.

Works with your agent

claude mcp add --scope user tinymuesli -- \
  /Applications/tinyMuesli.app/Contents/MacOS/tinyMuesli mcp
tinyMuesli is a menu bar app. Updates install from the app's menu.
